# 💙 PocketPilot – Student Budget Assistant

**Track. Manage. Save.**

PocketPilot is a web-based student budget management application designed to simplify personal expense tracking and financial planning. It enables students to monitor daily expenses, manage monthly budgets, split shared costs, and analyze spending patterns through organized summaries and visual insights.

## 🎯 Problem Statement

Students often struggle to manage limited budgets, track daily spending, and divide shared expenses. Lack of proper expense monitoring can lead to unnecessary spending and poor financial planning.

## 💡 Our Solution

PocketPilot provides a simple and organized platform that helps students maintain control over their finances through budget management, expense tracking, shared expense splitting, and spending analysis.

## ✨ Key Features

- **Budget Management:** Set monthly budgets and monitor remaining balances.
- **Daily Expense Tracking:** Record expenses with categories, amounts, and dates.
- **Expense Categorization:** Organize spending into food, travel, shopping, entertainment, and other categories.
- **Shared Expense Splitting:** Divide common expenses among friends.
- **Spending Visualization:** Understand spending patterns through charts and graphical summaries.
- **Financial Overview:** View total expenses, remaining budget, and category-wise spending.

## ⚙️ How It Works

1. **Open Application:** Access the PocketPilot dashboard.
2. **Set Monthly Budget:** Enter the available monthly budget.
3. **Add Daily Expenses:** Record expense amount, date, and category.
4. **Process Expense Data:** Calculate total spending and remaining balance.
5. **Split Shared Costs:** Divide expenses among multiple people.
6. **View Dashboard:** Analyze spending summaries and visual charts.
7. **Review & Plan:** Make informed financial decisions based on spending insights.

## 🧮 Basic Calculations

- **Total Expenses:** Sum of all recorded expenses.
- **Remaining Balance:** Monthly Budget − Total Expenses.
- **Split Amount:** Total Shared Expense ÷ Number of People.

## 🌟 Expected Benefits

- Better financial planning and budget control.
- Improved awareness of daily spending habits.
- Reduced unnecessary expenses.
- Simplified sharing of common expenses.
- Better understanding of financial patterns through visual insights.

## 🚀 Project Vision

PocketPilot aims to make personal budget management easier and more accessible for students by combining simple expense tracking with practical financial insights.
